Coming Soon

Pigeon is still being built. Everything on this page is planned and not built yet. You'll see some of it greyed out in the menus already.

Letters carried by friends [Not built yet]

A message you write while a friend is away already goes as a letter: sealed so only their devices can open it, and left at their Lighthouses until they're back. Later, a carrier, a friend you both have who happens to be online, will be able to carry it too, so it gets through even when no Lighthouse can be reached.

1 You write to Mika while she is offline. Pigeon seals it to Mika.
2 Alex, a friend of you both, is online: your Pigeon hands the letter to Alex as well as to the Lighthouses.
3 Alex keeps it, unable to read it, for up to 30 days.
4 When Mika and Alex are next online together, Alex delivers it, and drops it once Mika signs that she has it. 

     YOU                       ALEX (carrier)                    MIKA
      |                             |                         (offline)
      |--- sealed letter ---------->|                             .
      |    for Mika                 |  keeps it, can't read it    .
      |                             |  ...hours later...          |
      |                             |--- the letter ------------->|
      |                             |<-- "got it", signed --------|
      |                             |  drops its copy             |
Figure 7. A letter carried by a mutual friend while Mika is away. (Planned, not built yet.)

Pages on Pigeon Explorer [Not built yet]

Pigeon Explorer is a browser for pigeon:// addresses. These Help pages already come with Pigeon and open from this computer. Later, anyone will be able to make a homepage of their own, kept on their own computer and copied by friends who mirror it, so it stays up while its owner sleeps. A page nobody is hosting shows "page not hosted".

Pages are written in PigeonHTML: the HTML of the time, with tables, fonts and colours, and no scripts. They are kept small enough to load over a dial-up line, 100 KB each.

And more

  • Groups [Not built yet]: rooms for a few friends at once.
