Your Private Rooms

Every pair of friends has a room of their own on the relays. Only the two of you can find it, only the two of you can get in, and it moves every day.

A room named by a secret

When you add a friend, your two Pigeons agree on a pair secret: 32 random bytes that only you two hold. Your room's name is worked out from that secret and from the date, so:

  • nobody else can work out where you meet, even if they know who you both are;
  • the room has a new name every day, by the clock the whole world shares (UTC);
  • the room is locked with a password made from the same secret, so nobody can get in or read how your computers reach each other.

Chats, buzzes and "is writing..." all go through this room.

Around midnight

Two computers' clocks are never quite the same, and a new day starts at midnight UTC wherever you are. So for the last hour of each day your Pigeon also waits in tomorrow's room, and for the first hour of the day in yesterday's. Friends whose clocks are less than an hour apart still meet.

   UTC      22:00     23:00     00:00     01:00     02:00
              |         |         |         |         |
   Nov 24's room ===========================|
   Nov 25's room        |===================================...
                        |         |         |
                        |         |         +-- yesterday's room left
                        |         +-- the date changes
                        +-- tomorrow's room joined too
Figure 3. Today's room and tomorrow's overlap for two hours around midnight UTC.

Going through the relay

Normally your computer connects straight to your friend's whenever it can. That's fastest, but it means your friend's computer learns your IP address, the number your network goes by. If you'd rather it didn't, turn on the relay:

1 In Pigeon Messenger, open the File menu.
2 Choose Always Go Through the Relay. A tick shows it is on. (It is also under Connection in Settings.)
3 From now on every room, your private rooms and invites alike, goes through the relay only. 

   RELAY OFF:   you <=====================================> friend
                     direct; your friend sees your address
   RELAY ON:    you <=========> [ TURN relay ] <==========> friend
                     sealed packets; your friend never sees your
                     address, and the relay keeps nothing

Good to know
The relay is slower, and it has to be reachable. If it can't be reached, Pigeon doesn't quietly connect directly instead. It stays unconnected, and a balloon says the relay can't be reached and where to turn the setting off.
