Hello, and welcome to the Pigeonweb

The Pigeonweb is made of pages people keep on their own computers. There's no company in the middle holding everyone's pages. When you open a page, Pigeon Explorer asks the people who have it, the owner and any friends who keep a copy, and gets it straight from them.

That means a page is only up while somebody who has it is online. If nobody is, Explorer tells you so and you can try again later. That's how a web without servers works.

Getting around
  • Type an address in the Address bar and press Enter. Addresses start with pigeon:// and then a name, like pigeon://mika.
  • Back and Forward walk through the pages you've seen in this window.
  • Home always brings you back here.
  • New to Pigeon? Pigeon Help explains how it all works, and it lives on your computer too.
  • The bar along the bottom says where a page came from. This one says On this computer.

Try it
Here's a friend's homepage: pigeon://mika. Mika isn't online right now, so you'll see what Explorer says when nobody is hosting a page. Then come back with the Back button.
