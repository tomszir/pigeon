==============================================

0.7.1, 25 November 2026
----------------------------------------------
  * Fixed: after removing a friend who was
    offline, or reloading while leaving a
    Pigeon, it could refuse to start ("Pigeon
    couldn't start in this browser"). It
    starts again now, with nothing lost.
  * If Pigeon ever can't start, the whole
    screen says so, with Try Again, and
    Start Over to wipe this browser's
    Pigeon.
  * Leaving a Pigeon shows how many friends
    have heard you left, and Don't Wait goes
    on without the rest.

0.7.0, 25 November 2026
----------------------------------------------
  * Started Pigeon on your phone and your
    computer separately? Now you can make
    them one. Choose File, Link to My Other
    Device on the one you'll give up, and it
    offers to leave that Pigeon first: its
    friends are told you left, it's wiped,
    and it links to the one you keep.
  * Change your name or personal message on
    one device and your other devices take
    it too, the next time they're online
    together.
  * Friends now see your new name as soon as
    you change it, not after reconnecting.
  * Pigeon open in another tab? That tab now
    says so across the whole screen, with
    one button to use Pigeon there instead.

0.6.0, 25 November 2026
----------------------------------------------
  * Tell your devices apart by fingerprint in
    My Devices.
  * Notice a copied browser profile and offer
    a fresh start.
  * Save your data to a sealed file and import
    it elsewhere.
  * Save a conversation as a text file.
  * Bring your Pigeon back with a recovery
    key.
  * Write pigeon's help pages.
  * Open pages that come with pigeon.
  * Keep pages to 2003's html and css.
  * Open pigeon help from the menu and the
    start page.
  * Stack pages into one column on the pocket.
  * Line up a stacked table's cells in one
    column.
  * Fit the pocket's toolbar and address in
    one row.
  * Step back inside pigeon with the browser's
    back.
  * Give lone links a finger's height in one
    column.

0.5.0, 25 November 2026
----------------------------------------------
  * Every friend has a pigeon of their own: a
    little bird drawn from their key, the same
    on every Pigeon, next to their name.
  * Use Pigeon on your phone and your laptop
    as the same you. On your first device,
    choose File, Link a Device, and type the
    code on the new one. Your friends come
    along, and they still see one of you.
  * Friends now meet in a private room only
    the two of you can find, so nobody
    watching can tell when you talk, or join
    in. Friends you already had move there
    the next time you're both online.
  * File, Always Go Through the Relay keeps
    your address from friends, at the cost of
    a slower connection.
  * On an iPhone, Pigeon shows how to add it
    to the Home Screen, so Safari doesn't
    forget you after 7 days away.
  * After an update, Pigeon tells you once in
    a balloon. Click it to see what changed.
  * Changelog.txt on the desktop lists what's
    new in each version, newest first.

0.4.0, 24 November 2026
----------------------------------------------
  * Adding a friend shows each step as it
    happens: reaching the relays, finding your
    friend's Pigeon and asking who they are.
    If a step stalls, Pigeon says what to
    check.
  * Once you're friends, the chat opens
    straight away, starting with the day you
    became friends.
  * Every friend has a menu: right-click them,
    press and hold on a phone, or press
    Shift+F10. See their profile, give them a
    name of your own, copy their fingerprint,
    mute them for a while, or remove them.
  * Removing a friend tells their Pigeon, so
    they aren't left waiting for you. You can
    block them too, and unblock them later
    from Contacts > Blocked People.

0.3.0, 24 November 2026
----------------------------------------------
  * Friends on different networks can reach
    each other, such as a phone on mobile data
    and a laptop on home Wi-Fi. When a direct
    connection is blocked, Pigeon passes your
    messages through a relay that can't read
    them.

0.2.0, 24 November 2026
----------------------------------------------
  * Pick a name, invite a friend and chat
    browser to browser: messages, buzzes,
    "is writing..." and away or busy.
  * Invite with a link, a QR code, or a short
    code your friend can type in.
  * Chats are kept in this browser and catch
    up when you're both online.
  * On a phone, Pigeon runs as the Kestrel
    Pocket, a handheld with its own Today
    screen.
  * Turn Pigeon off, or leave it alone for a
    while, and it sleeps behind a screen
    saver: pigeons in flight or a brick maze.
    A click or a key wakes it with everything
    where you left it.
  * About Pigeon says which version you have.
