# 0047: Replacing secrets from before sealed pairing

Every pair secret and self secret kept from before Pigeon sealed the secrets it hands over is replaced once, by a new one sent as sealed mail to the devices that hold the old, with the earliest proposal winning where several meet.

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-26

## Context

Before [0046](0046-codes-through-spake2.md), a typed code or the code behind an `#i/` or `#l/` link could be cracked from what the relays publish, and nothing bound pairing or linking to the connection, so someone in the middle could read the pair secret in `pair-accept` or the whole link bundle (the self secret and every pair secret). 0046 stopped new leaks and left the old secrets for later. Friends who moved off the room named from both ids ([0026](0026-pair-rooms-from-a-shared-secret.md)) were sent their secret in that room, which anyone knowing both ids could enter, so theirs may be known too.

Whoever holds a pair secret can compute the pair rooms and mail tags. With hellos bound to their channel ([0043](0043-hellos-bound-to-their-channel.md)) they can't pass for anyone or read chat, but they can sit in the rooms and see when the two are online, and from where unless they use the relay, and see that mail waits for them. The self secret gives the same view of your own devices.

Constraints: no construction of our own where a standard one fits; long-lived keys stay in WebCrypto; a friend whose Pigeon hasn't updated must keep working; anything compared has one encoding; nothing secret may go through the rooms the old secret opens.

0026's move off the id-named room is the model: a one-time move, made the next time both are around, with the old place kept until the friend is known to have moved. That move ran on a live session in the old room. This one can't: whoever knows the old secret can be in that room.

## Decision

- **Mark what's from before.** A friend's record carries `sealed: true` once its secret was made on this device or reached it sealed (a live code, an invite link, a link bundle or a replacement); the self secret's state carries the same. Anything without the mark is replaced. That includes secrets from 0.8.1, which were already safe: no stored value tells them apart, a date would depend on the clocks, and replacing a safe secret costs one letter each way.
- **Sealed mail only.** The new secret goes in a `mail:rekey` letter sealed to each of the other side's devices still on their list, by the sealing keys those devices signed ([0037](0037-the-lighthouse-and-its-mailbox.md)), under the old secret's tags, which is all a Pigeon that hasn't moved looks under. A copy goes to your own other devices. This is the existing mailbox: sealing, signatures, receipts, putting again every 3 days for 30 days; there is no new cryptography, only the device's signing key signing the proposal as it signs its sealing key.
- **Either side starts; the earliest wins.** Each Pigeon proposes at a mail check, after taking what's waiting, so it takes a proposal already there instead of making a rival one. Where proposals for the same old secret meet, the one with the earliest `at` wins, then the lower proposing device, then the lower secret, compared as bytes. A device answers a proposal that loses with the winner, and whenever its own winner changes it sends it to both sides, so a device the proposer couldn't seal to still hears it from another, and all of them end on the same one in whatever order letters arrive.
- **Only devices still on a list choose.** A proposal is signed by the device that made it, over the old secret's name, the new secret, its time and its maker, with its own label, and is kept and passed on with that signature, so a device can't put another's name or an earlier time on its own. Each letter carries the device logs its sender knows for both sides, merged before anything is acted on, and a proposal from a device no longer on its side's list never wins. Without that, a device removed from your list, while a friend hadn't heard of the removal yet, could propose with the earliest time and lead both of you to rooms it knows.
- **Move once the other side is seen, and wait for all of it.** A device joins the new rooms when it has a winner and keeps the old rooms and mail until it sees a device on the other side holding the new secret, by a `mail:rekey` from it naming it or a bound hello in one of its rooms. It then leaves the old rooms and mails under the new secret, but keeps fetching the old secret's tags until every device on the other side's list has been seen. A fixed time wouldn't do: a tablet left off for longer than mail is kept would come back to nobody listening, and stay apart for good. Letting the old secret go keeps which proposal won: a proposal that beats it can still come later, from a device on the same side whose letter was held up, and a device that had dropped the record would ignore it and stay apart from everyone who took it. A device whose winner changes sends it under the tags of the replacement it held too, where devices that let the old secret go look.
- **The self secret the same way**, between your own devices. One never handed to another device is just marked; one whose other devices are all gone is replaced on its own. My Devices then asks you to check every device on your list is yours, since a device linked through someone in the middle is on it and gets the new secret like the rest.

## Consequences

Once both sides move, someone who learned an old secret has an empty room and old tags: the new rooms and tags can't be worked out from it, and the letters under the old tags are sealed to device keys they don't have. A friend whose Pigeon hasn't updated keeps chatting in the old rooms, where the updated side waits for them, and moves when it updates. Until both sides are seen with the new secret, whoever knows the old one still sees them in the old rooms, as before; that lasts until the other side's next visit.

The costs: one letter each way per device, a few more when both propose at once, and one more tag fetched per friend until all their devices are seen, for good with a device they never open again and never remove. A Lighthouse that knows the old secret sees that tag asked for, and so which connection is checking that friendship's mail, as it could before. A device of theirs that hasn't updated while another has moved hears nothing new until it updates, and then catches up. A device someone linked in the middle and you didn't remove gets the new self secret; removing it shuts it out of nothing it already holds, which is the open question of replacing secrets when a device is removed, still left for later. A friend whose Lighthouses this Pigeon may not reach isn't sent a proposal until it can, though theirs is still taken. A device the friend removed before the replacement, while you hadn't heard of the removal, is on the list you seal to, so it's sent the new secret; that is the same open question.

Ruled out:
- **Sending it in the old pair room**, as the move off the id-named room did: whoever holds the old secret can be there.
- **Deriving the new secret from the old**: whoever holds the old one derives it too.
- **Only the lower id starts**, as 0026 did: a friend who can't be mailed, or hasn't updated, would hold the other side back forever.
- **First come, first kept, per device**: with two devices on one side, both could propose and their friend could keep a different one on each.
- **A key exchange of our own**: devices already have signed sealing keys and a mailbox that carries sealed letters, so a fresh random secret sealed to them needs nothing new.
