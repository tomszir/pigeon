# 0051: Replacing secrets after a removal

Removing a device replaces the self secret and every pair secret, each by a new round of the sealed `mail:rekey` replacement, begun for the secret held now and carrying the round before's winner so devices still in that round can follow, and closes every invite link open then.

- Status: accepted; the invite links part (closing every link open at a removal, and the links to close kept until a closed card for each is up) is superseded by [0060](0060-invite-links-bound-to-their-maker-for-a-week.md): a removal closes no link, since only a link's maker counts and a link ends within 7 days
- Date: 2026-11-26

## Context

A removed device keeps the self secret and every pair secret it was given ([0024](0024-a-signed-list-of-devices.md), [0026](0026-pair-rooms-from-a-shared-secret.md)). Its hello no longer counts ([0043](0043-hellos-bound-to-their-channel.md)), but with the secrets it can still work out your self room, every pair room and every mail tag: see when you and your friends are online and from where, unless you use the relay, get the device logs a connection is sent before its hello, and see the sealed mail that waits. Removal is what someone does after losing a device, so it's when they expect it to lose that.

[0047](0047-replacing-secrets-from-before-sealed-pairing.md) replaces secrets from before sealed pairing with new ones sealed to devices, as `mail:rekey`, with the earliest proposal for an old secret winning. It left replacing secrets on a removal for later. Reusing it is the obvious carrier, with one catch: a device keeps the round it settled, old secret and winner, so a proposal from a later round that still lands, or one held up, is still taken. A removal proposing in that kept round would name the original old secret and lose to the winner, which the removed device may hold.

Constraints: no construction of our own where a standard one fits; long-lived keys stay in WebCrypto; a friend on an older Pigeon keeps working; anything compared has one encoding; nothing secret goes through rooms the old secret opens.

## Decision

- **The device that removes marks, then replaces.** The writer that removes a device, or the device a recovery that leaves others off makes the writer, marks the self secret and every pair secret `renew` and checks mail. It begins a round for each at that check, or the next one that has someone to seal to. A friend added from an export is marked too, since the file, or a device it came from, may have given its secret away. Only that device marks: the others learn of the removal from the log, and if each began a round, every removal would cost several. The device notes which removals it has acted on only after the marks are kept, and on each start acts on any in its log it hasn't, so a tab that closes between keeping the log and marking still marks.
- **A new round, for the secret held now.** The proposal's `replaces` names the secret the device holds, not the one the last round replaced, so it doesn't compete with that round's winner. The round under way becomes the round before, kept as `prior`. A device takes a proposal naming the secret it holds as a new round, when its maker is on its side's list, and still takes proposals for its round under way as 0047 does.
- **Until a round begins from it.** The new round can still be won by a proposal for the same secret that the other side made before it heard of the removal, with an earlier time, and that proposal was sealed to the removed device. So the device that began the round keeps what it proposed as `begun`. If it takes a winner that isn't that, by any of the ways below, and the winner's round didn't begin from it, it marks the secret to renew again and begins a new round from that winner. A round that began from `begun` was proposed by a device that held it, which only devices still on the lists were sealed, with the logs that say who's off, so it's taken as it is and `begun` is dropped. Two sides that both removed a device and began at once end after one more round: the one that lost begins from the other's winner, and the other takes that round as begun from its own.
- **The round before's winner rides along.** A new round's letter carries that winner's proposal, signed by its own maker, whose secret the new round's `replaces` names. It lets a device that isn't on that secret follow: one still in the round before, whatever it chose there, and one in a rival new round begun from another of that round's proposals, which compares the two as proposals in one round are compared. A proposal for the round before, with no way further, is answered with the winner and its prior, under the tags of the secret it proposed too. A device fetches the round before's old tags until its own round is let go, so it hears those devices.
- **Where to wait.** A device keeps the round before's rooms and mail as well while neither round's secret has been seen held by the other side, so a friend who never moved off the round before still meets it.
- **Older Pigeons.** One from before 0047 leaves `mail:rekey` at the Lighthouse and finds it when it updates, as with 0047. One with 0047 but not this takes the letter, can't use it, and gives its receipt, so the letter isn't put again. So a device that meets one of the other side's devices in the old rooms while nobody there has been seen with the new secret sends the round again to that device, once for each device and winner, noted with the round across restarts only once a Lighthouse took the letter, so a send that failed is tried at the next meeting.
- **Invite links.** Your devices tell each other the secrets of the invite links they make, so a removed device knows every link open when it was removed, and the cards of those links name its sealing key. Each of your devices still on the list that hears of a removal closes every link open then: its own as Cancel does, telling whoever is waiting, and those your other devices made with a closed card of its own. A card on a newer list wins over one on an older, so a card the removed device puts on the list from before loses. Replacing a link's secret instead would change the link people were given, which closing does too, with one rule less. A link made after the removal is one the removed device never heard of. The links to close are kept in a list until a closed card for each is up: for this device's own, at every Lighthouse it puts cards at and every one that held one of its cards; for another device's, at each of this device's Lighthouses, putting one only where none of its own is up yet. A card's id is forgotten only once a newer card of the link is up at that Lighthouse or the Lighthouse says the card is gone, so a Lighthouse that's down when a card is put again or closed still has its old card replaced later. What's left is tried at each mail check and start, so a Lighthouse that was down, or a tab that closed, doesn't leave a link open.
- **No new cryptography.** The proposal, signatures, sealing and mailbox are 0047's; the only new field is `prior`, an existing proposal checked as one.

## Consequences

Once a friend's devices have taken the new pair secret, the removed device has old rooms and tags with nobody in them, and every letter under those tags that it could open names a secret nobody ends on. Your own devices move to a new self room the same way. A friend whose Pigeon doesn't replace secrets after a removal keeps chatting in the old rooms, where your devices wait for them, and the removed device can still see both of you online there until they update.

The costs: one letter each way per device and friend per removal, and one more round when both sides removed a device at once; one more tag fetched per friend while a round is unsettled after one before it; one more letter per winner to a friend still on an older Pigeon who's met in the old rooms. Every invite link open at a removal closes, so you make new ones.

Limits:
- Only two rounds are kept. A device two rounds behind (away through two removals, longer than mail is kept) hears nothing it can follow and stays apart until it's paired again. Rounds nested three deep can leave devices apart the same way, since the answer to the round before reaches only one round back; and where a removed device signed such rounds before its removal, and the other side removed a device at the same time, the devices left apart can end on a secret it knows. That takes three removals close together with the other side's writer behind.
- `begun` can outlast the secret it names: `propose` copies it, and it travels in a link bundle. The only effect is one more round.
- A proposal from before a removal, sealed to the removed device and held up past the removal, can still win the round before and move a device still there to a secret the removed device may know, until that device follows the new round. In the round the removal began, `begun` covers it only until a round begins from `begun`: one held up past that, for the secret before, is answered and never taken.
- Only the device that made the removal begins rounds. If it never comes back to a mail check, nothing replaces the secrets, and the other devices don't take over.
- A friend this Pigeon can't seal to (no sealing key known, or no Lighthouse it may reach) is skipped at each check until it can, with nothing shown to you meanwhile.
- The `renew` mark is kept with the secret and goes in a link bundle, so a device linked before the round begins begins one too: an extra round, nothing broken.
- A request that came through a link before it closed was sealed to every device on the list then, the removed one included, and while the link's owner is offline it isn't closed by the owner. Another device's closed card goes up at this device's Lighthouses, so not at a Lighthouse that only the owner's list names.
- Closing links is done as well as it can be, not to the end. A card of a link at a Lighthouse that was down when the list changed, or that has left the list since, isn't closed there or taken down, and stays as it was. A request waiting on a link closed while its Lighthouse was down, or just before a tab closed, may never be answered `closed`. A closed card put for another device's link whose answer was lost isn't known to be up, so it's put again. These are left for a follow-up.
- A removed device still holds a closed link's secret, so it can still put cards under the link's tag, and whoever opens the link reads only so many of them. Closing the link doesn't stop that; putting cards claimed by the link's owner, so an opener reads those first, does (GHSA-q2h9).
- A removal made before a device ran this is taken as already acted on, the first time it runs, so the secrets it left aren't replaced and its links aren't closed. A removal made after is acted on even if a tab closes in between: what it calls for is noted as done only once it's kept, and tried again at the next change or start if it fails.
- A self secret with no other device left is replaced on this device alone, as in 0047.

Ruled out:
- **Proposing in the kept round with the current time**: it loses to the kept winner, which the removed device may know.
- **Proposing in the kept round with a time before the winner's**: it would win, also on Pigeons with only 0047, but the rooms and mail of that round point at its original old secret, not the one the other side is on, and the time would no longer say when it was made.
- **Every device that hears of a removal starting a round**: each removal would cost one round per device, and a device that heard late would replace a secret already replaced.
- **Deriving the new secret from the old**: the removed device would derive it too.
