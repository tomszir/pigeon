# Roadmap

The milestones in build order, and the check that says each one is done.

| Milestone             | Scope                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Done when                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| M1 · Desktop shell    | Stage and scaling, window manager, taskbar, menu, tray and balloons, the UI kit from the canvas's Components board, Read Me window                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | the canvas's Desktop board works offline with no P2P at all                                                                  |
| M2 · Two tabs talking | Identity, pairing by link and QR, pair rooms, Messenger with buzz and typing; adds Trystero and Playwright                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | two browsers on different networks chat                                                                                      |
| M12 · What's new      | Changelog.txt on the desktop: notes written in plain words for each release, newest first, saying what changed for the people using Pigeon (not commit subjects); a balloon after an update, "Pigeon was updated to 0.4.0. See what's new?", that opens it; none on a first visit                                                                                                      | after a release, a returning visitor is told once and reads what changed without knowing what a commit is                    |
| M10 · Avatars         | A pixel pigeon for everyone, drawn from the hash of their id from parts drawn for Pigeon (plumage, markings, eyes, accessory, tile), the same on every Pigeon and never sent; shown in the buddy list, chats, profiles and pairing, beside the fingerprint, never instead of it                                                                                                        | two Pigeons draw the same bird for Mika, and the pairing screen shows it next to her fingerprint                             |
| M13 · Your devices    | Each device keeps its own key, tied to one identity by a signed log of devices ([0024](decisions/0024-a-signed-list-of-devices.md)); linking a phone, laptop or desktop in person by QR code and a short code; removing a lost one from any other; friends see one friend; the friends list comes along when linking, and chats catch up through pair rooms                            | Toms links his phone to his laptop, Mika sees one Toms, and a message sent from the phone shows on the laptop too            |
| M7 · Async invites    | Several open invites at once, each kept for 7 days; links and QR codes pair through a sealed mailbox (the Lighthouse post office, a Worker route) that sees only opaque blobs and deletes them on pickup or expiry; typed codes stay live; the sealing keypair; a plain privacy page in Read Me and a "Live invites only" setting                                                      | Mika opens Toms's link while Toms is offline, and they're friends once Toms opens Pigeon, without ever being online together |
| M11 · Desktop life    | Desktop icons that can be selected with a rubber band, dragged, arranged and kept; a Desktop tab in Display Properties with several wallpapers, a solid colour, positions and your own picture kept in this browser; cursors drawn for Pigeon with a flapping-pigeon busy cursor; Coo the pigeon delivering balloons, now and then with a joke, never while you type, and easy to hide | a returning visitor finds their icons where they left them, and can send Coo away for good                                   |
| M3 · Files            | Content-addressed chunks (named by their hash, served by anyone who holds them), progress, accept and decline, resume, "Send when they come online", a picture chooser and your own picture (48×48, dithered, sent as a small file); pairing always shows the M10 bird                                                                                                                 | a 50 MB file arrives intact                                                                                                  |
| M4 · Letters          | Sealing, carriers, delivery, receipts, Letters in Flight                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | A → C → B works with A and B never online together                                                                           |
| M8 · Groups           | Shared spaces (a room, a member list, a signed log and an ephemeral channel) that pair chats move onto; private groups of up to 12 with an owner-signed membership log; group invites through the M7 mailbox; group files from M3's chunks                                                                                                                                             | a member who was offline catches up from any other member, and a removed member gets nothing new                             |
| M9 · Games            | Pigeon Games: turn-based games as signed move logs on spaces, played live or a move at a time; commit-reveal dice; original and traditional games with nothing hidden; "Invite to a game" from a chat or group                                                                                                                                                                         | two friends finish a game across separate visits, and it replays from its log                                                |
| M5 · Pigeon Explorer  | Resolver, page host and mirrors, page snapshots as lists of M3's chunks, page builder, PigeonHTML, widgets, webrings and a portal, the "not hosted" page                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Mika's page loads from a mirror while Mika is offline                                                                        |
| M6 · Always on        | Lighthouse peer, lobby, public groups with moderation designed first, starter pages, Coo as a bot (M11 gave him his body)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | a first-time visitor alone at 3 a.m. still has something to do                                                               |

Milestones keep their numbers when the order changes, so a decision that names one still means the same milestone.

Before code: M7's mailbox is a server of ours that holds sealed blobs, which the brief's rules don't allow today, so a decision has to accept it and the brief changes with it. M8's shared spaces get a decision too. M13 follows [0024](decisions/0024-a-signed-list-of-devices.md), which is accepted when M13 starts; it comes before M7 so invites and letters are sealed to every device from the start, and M8 adds the self space that syncs your own data between devices. M12's release notes need a decision that supersedes the "no changelog file" part of [0021](decisions/0021-releases-with-release-please.md): notes for people are written for each release and shipped with the app, and git history stays the developers' change log. M10's birds, M11's cursors and Coo are drawn for Pigeon, never copied ([0007](decisions/0007-original-and-openly-licensed-assets-only.md)). Later, not yet scheduled: message boards on the peer net after M5 and M6 (public spaces with threads ordered by bump, an owner's moderation log and proof-of-work per post; pseudonymous, never anonymous, and text only, with no image uploads), real-time games, games made by other people (sandboxed, ask first), and hidden-hand card games.

Current state: M1 is built. The desktop boots offline with Read Me open; the Messenger shows the real, empty state, and `?demo` (or "See a sample conversation") plays the Desktop board's sample, labelled as such. Letters, Shared Files and Pigeon Explorer open an in-world "still being built" message. Window positions and settings live in memory only until M2 adds storage.

M2 is built, pending its done check across two real networks. Each visitor gets a WebCrypto identity kept in IndexedDB; Add a Friend shares a one-use invite as a code to type, a short link and a QR code, and adds a friend only after showing who answered and their fingerprint; the Messenger lists real friends with presence, and conversations carry messages, buzzes and "is writing" (shown as the transcript's last line) over Trystero, note when the friend comes online or goes offline, and keep chats and catch them up between visits. The sample is still there, labelled as such. `mise run e2e` pairs and chats two isolated browsers over the public relays. Shell settings and window positions still live in memory only. On phones Pigeon runs on the Kestrel Pocket ([0019](decisions/0019-the-kestrel-pocket-phone-layout.md)).
