# Glossary

The words Pigeon uses for its own things.

- **Buzz**: shaking a friend's conversation window to get their attention.
- **Carrier**: a peer holding a sealed letter for someone else.
- **Device**: one browser's key. Several devices can be one person, on a device log.
- **Device log**: the signed chain of entries saying which devices speak for an identity.
- **Epoch**: a stretch of a device log between recoveries; a recovery key starts the next one.
- **Fingerprint**: 16 hex characters from a peer's key, compared out loud or on screen when pairing.
- **Changelog.txt**: the release notes on the desktop, written for the people using Pigeon, newest first.
- **Coo**: the opt-in bot that runs in your own tab.
- **Invite**: an 8-character code, or a short link and QR code carrying it, that pairs one friend with you within 30 minutes while your Pigeon is open.
- **Invite link**: a link that waits at your Lighthouses for up to 30 days and lets in up to 25 people, each once you add them, without either of you having to be online at the same time.
- **Letter**: a message sealed to an offline friend and carried until they return.
- **Lighthouse**: the always-on peer. Same protocol as everyone, labelled as such in the UI.
- **Linking**: putting a new device on your device log, in person, by code and a 6-digit check.
- **Lobby**: the opt-in public room of strangers who are online.
- **Mirror**: a peer that keeps and serves a copy of someone's page.
- **Monitor**: the beige Kestrel CRT drawn around the stage. Kestrel is a made-up maker.
- **One Column**: Pigeon Explorer's view that stacks a page's tables to a phone's width, as handheld browsers of 2003 did; the Pocket's default.
- **On this computer**: where Pigeon Explorer's status bar says a page came from when it's one bundled with Pigeon, such as the start page at `pigeon://home`.
- **Pair room**: the private room two friends meet in, named and locked by their pair secret and renamed every UTC day.
- **Pair secret**: 32 random bytes two friends share from pairing, which only they hold.
- **Petname**: what you call a contact, which only you see.
- **PigeonHTML**: the 2000s-only HTML/CSS subset pages are written in.
- **Pigeonweb**: all the `pigeon://` pages together.
- **Recovery key**: a key kept on paper or in a file that brings your Pigeon back with every device lost, and outranks every device.
- **Stage**: the logical screen everything is drawn on, at the chosen resolution (1024×768 unless changed).
